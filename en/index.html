About

TAP Institute

TAP Institute is a spatial analysis company specializing in satellite imagery analysis, spatial analytics consulting and GeoAI education.

Building on long experience in GIS, remote sensing and geospatial policy research and education, we work with public institutions from analysis to training, so that satellite and spatial data can be put to use in real policy and in the field.

Services

Services

We analyze satellite and spatial data and deliver results that can be used in policy and in the field.

Satellite Imagery Analysis

위성영상 분석

Processing and analysis of optical and SAR satellite imagery.

  • InSAR-based ground subsidence and surface deformation analysis
  • Disaster response analysis, including floods and dam releases
  • Land use and agricultural remote sensing

Spatial Analytics Consulting

공간분석 컨설팅

Building the evidence base for policies and projects with spatial data.

  • GIS-based site selection and spatial analysis
  • Research projects for public institutions
  • Geospatial policy advisory, ODA and international cooperation project planning

R&D and Data Services

연구개발·데이터 서비스

Developing spatial analysis technology and delivering results as data and services.

  • Development and demonstration of AI-based land and ground change detection
  • Government and local government R&D projects
  • Spatial database development, web maps and monitoring dashboards

GeoAI Education and Content

GeoAI 교육·콘텐츠

Teaching geospatial data and AI for practical use, and creating educational content.

  • Hands-on GIS training, including QGIS
  • Satellite image processing and AI analysis training
  • Professional training and university courses
  • Online courses, videos and other geospatial learning content

Approach

Approach

We start from the purpose of each project and finish with results that can be used on the ground.

  1. Scoping

    Define the purpose and scope of the analysis with the client.

  2. Data

    Collect and prepare satellite imagery and spatial data.

  3. Analysis

    Analyze with GIS, remote sensing and AI methods.

  4. Delivery

    Deliver maps, dashboards and reports, with training where needed.
